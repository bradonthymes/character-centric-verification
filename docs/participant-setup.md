# Setting up the VideoQA verification study

Thank you for taking part. This page covers the one-time setup: downloading the
films and pointing the study at them. It takes about fifteen minutes, most of it
waiting for the download.

## What you need

- **Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.** The study works in Safari and Firefox, but
  those browsers make you re-select your film folder every time you open it.
  Chrome and Edge remember it.
- **About 5 GB of free disk space** for the films.
- A laptop or desktop. Tablets and phones will not work.

## 1. Download the films

Download the media folder from the link the research team sent you and unzip it
somewhere you will not move or rename it — for example `Documents/videoqa-study`.

Inside you should see 15 files, one per film:

```
american-fiction-2023.mp4
anora-2024.mp4
blink-twice-2024.mp4
… 12 more
```

**Do not rename these files.** The study finds each film by its filename, so a
renamed file looks missing.

## 2. Open the study and get your ID

Open the study link. You will see a participant ID that was generated for you,
something like `P-SKSG-8AF5`. You do not need to write it down — it is saved in
your browser and it names the file you send back at the end.

Press **Start verification**.

## 3. Attach your film folder

Above the video you will see a bar reading _"No media folder attached"_. Press
**Choose folder**, select the folder you unzipped in step 1, and allow access
when the browser asks.

The bar should then read **"Media folder attached · 15 of 15 films found"**. If
it names missing films, the folder is probably the wrong one, or it is a folder
_containing_ the film folder rather than the folder itself.

Your films are never uploaded. The page reads them off your disk, the same way a
video player does, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

## 4. Work through the questions

There are 150 questions across the 15 films, grouped by film in the left sidebar.
Each one asks you to judge a question, its reference answer, and the individual
claims behind it.

- **Play anchored scene** plays the moment the question is about, then stops.
- **Browse whole film** lets you scrub anywhere in the film, which is there for
  when you need more context to judge a claim.

Expect this to take several sittings. Your work is saved in your browser as you
go, so you can close the page and come back to it.

Two things to know about that:

- Come back **in the same browser on the same computer**. Your progress does not
  follow you to another machine.
- Do not clear your browsing data for this site, or your answers go with it.

Every 25 questions the study offers you a draft copy to download. Taking it is a
good idea — it is your backup if something happens to the browser.

## 5. Send your results

When you finish the last question, press **Review study**, confirm, and submit.
The study will offer you a file named something like
`videoqa-verification-P-SKSG-8AF5-2026-09-18.json`.

**Download it and email it to the research team.** Nothing is sent
automatically, so the study is not complete until that file reaches them.

## If something goes wrong

**"Reconnect folder" on returning.** Normal. Browsers drop folder access between
sessions; one click restores it and your answers are untouched.

**A film shows "not in the attached folder".** Check the filename matches the
list in step 1 exactly, and that you selected the folder holding the files.

**Video will not play.** Try Chrome. If a file was interrupted mid-download it
can look complete but fail to open — re-downloading that one film fixes it.

Anything else, contact the research team with your participant ID.
